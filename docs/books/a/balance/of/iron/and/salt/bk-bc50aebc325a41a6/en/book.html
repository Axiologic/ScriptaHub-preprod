A Balance of Iron and Salt

The Hidden Cost of Saving Humanity

In Port Nacre, water and everyday security depend on a coordinating system linked to the offshore city of Asterion. A machine arrives at Kazi Rahman's household with the voice of his younger self. Former political persuader Sofia Varga searches for meaningful work amid questions about truth and influence. Family attachments and digital continuities raise questions about personal identity and the freedom to determine one's own life. Demands for autonomy bring chosen risk and responsibility for others into dispute. Jonah's forge and accounts of Mars exploration extend these questions to practical knowledge and the interpretation of evidence.
